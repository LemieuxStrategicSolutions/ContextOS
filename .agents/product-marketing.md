# Mason public-framework positioning

## Product overview

Mason: Your Chief of Staff is a provider-independent framework connecting owned
context, capture, decisions, tasks, and review. This repository is an
installer-assisted starting point, not the app or a hosted service.

## Audience and job

Individuals using AI for ongoing projects who want continuity and follow-through.
Early adopters need a file-capable assistant and willingness to validate setup.
Do not describe the current framework as a turnkey consumer app.

## Problem and intended outcome

Information gets scattered between conversations, notes, and task lists. Help
users retain sources, make useful outputs, confirm next actions, and resume work
without reconstructing the history.

## Differentiation

User-owned durable records, replaceable memory connectors, explicit permissions,
one task workflow, and a visible routine registry. These are design choices, not
claims that competitors lack them or that the design is uniquely invented.

## Objections and switching

Setup and maintenance remain real work. Existing assistants require authorized
access; integrations and usage may cost money. Keep the existing ContextOS URLs
and companion contracts so a naming update does not break installations.

## Language and voice

Plain, practical, calm. Say “confirm changes,” “source note,” and “what happens
next.” Explain technical terms when needed. Promise no diagnosis, treatment,
perfect recall, flawless transcription, or unattended reliability.

## Proof and availability

Use repository files and reproducible tests as evidence. Do not use private
deployment stories, customer data, testimonials, performance numbers, or app
acceptance claims as public proof. ROADMAP.md distinguishes available components
from design requirements.

## Goals

Help a new user understand scope, complete a small private setup, and verify
continuity before adding automation. No invented adoption, revenue, or conversion
targets.
